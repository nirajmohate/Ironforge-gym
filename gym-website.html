Our Story

Built on Passion

IronForge Gym was founded in 2014 in the heart of Nashik with one simple mission — make world-class fitness accessible to everyone. What started as a small 2,000 sq ft facility has grown into a 10,000+ sq ft premium fitness destination serving 1,500+ members across Nashik.

Our founder, Vikram Patil, a national-level powerlifter, believed that every person deserves a gym that treats them like a champion — regardless of their current fitness level. That philosophy is alive in every corner of IronForge.

Our Mission
To empower every individual in Nashik to discover their strongest self through expert guidance, world-class facilities, and an inspiring community.
Our Vision
To become Maharashtra's most trusted fitness destination, where science, passion and community converge to create extraordinary transformations.
Our Values
Integrity, inclusivity, excellence and continuous improvement — we hold ourselves to the same standards we expect from our members.
